Key facts to sort out before a project

A commercial roof project can involve assessment, a defined scope, and a plan for carrying out the agreed work. The building’s layout, roof condition, access, and the extent of the work can all shape the discussion.

  • What it covers: Ask which roof sections and tasks are included, and whether the proposal addresses the concern you reported.
  • Typical timing: The schedule varies with the assessment, project size, access, materials, and provider availability. Ask for the expected sequence and timing for your building instead of relying on a general estimate.
  • What affects cost: Roof area, existing condition, selected materials, access needs, and the amount of work can affect the overall cost. Request an itemized explanation of the proposed scope and any exclusions.
  • How to compare providers: Give providers the same project details, then compare their descriptions of the work, assumptions, exclusions, and communication plan.

Some terms you may hear are worth clarifying. Roof system refers to the assembled components that make up the roof. Access describes how workers and materials can reach the work area. Proposal is the written description of planned work and its terms. Ask for plain-language explanations when a proposal uses unfamiliar terms.
