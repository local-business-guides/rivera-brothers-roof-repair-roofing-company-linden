Key facts about roof work

Roofing work starts with understanding the concern and the roof area involved. The details below can help you prepare for a conversation:

  • What it covers: A contractor may assess a reported issue, describe the work being proposed, and clarify which roof areas are included.
  • Typical timing: Timing depends on the need for an assessment, the size and nature of the work, scheduling, and any materials or access requirements. Ask for an estimate of the steps and timing for your specific project.
  • What affects cost: The roof’s size, condition, access, materials, and the amount of work can influence the total. Request a written breakdown so you can see what the proposed work covers.
  • How to compare providers: Ask each provider to describe the same scope, explain exclusions, and identify how you will receive updates if the plan changes.

You may hear a few common terms. A roof inspection is an assessment of the roof’s condition. Scope of work means the tasks included in a proposal. Materials refers to the roofing products selected for the job. Ask the contractor to explain any term that is unclear.
