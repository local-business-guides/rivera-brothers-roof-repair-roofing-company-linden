What the work covers and how to plan it

Roofing work may start with a review of the roof and a discussion of the problem or project goal. The contractor can then describe the recommended work, materials, and sequence. The specific scope depends on what the roof needs; don’t assume a small visible problem tells the whole story.

Key facts to discuss:
- What it covers: Ask which areas and tasks are included, and what is excluded.
- Typical timing: The time needed depends on the scope, the roof’s condition, material availability, and scheduling. Ask for an expected start and duration for your project.
- Cost factors: The work required, roof size and shape, material choices, and any additional repairs can affect the overall cost. Request an itemized explanation rather than relying on a general estimate.
- Provider comparison: Share the same project details with each provider, then compare scope, materials, timing, and credentials side by side.

A scope of work is the written description of planned tasks. An estimate is the provider’s projected cost for that scope. Ask how either document will be updated if the plan changes.
