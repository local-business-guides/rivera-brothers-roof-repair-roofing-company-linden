What to expect and what to compare

A repair discussion can begin with your description of the issue, followed by a review of the affected area and an explanation of the proposed work. The appropriate repair depends on what is found. Before approving a job, ask which area will be addressed, what materials are included, and how the contractor will handle additional damage if it becomes apparent during the work.

Key facts for a repair:
- What it covers: Confirm the specific repair area, the work included, and any related items that are not part of the proposal.
- Typical timing: Scheduling and completion depend on the repair scope, material availability, and conditions at the property. Request an expected start and duration for your job.
- Cost factors: The size and location of the repair, access to the roof, material needs, and hidden damage can affect the amount. Ask which assumptions the estimate is based on.

To compare providers, review proposals for the same work rather than comparing totals alone. Look for a written description, clear material details, a way to approve changes, and an explanation of the contractor’s findings. If something is unclear, ask for it to be put in plain language before you decide.

Useful terms include flashing, the material installed around roof edges and openings to help direct water; leak, water entering where it should not; and decking, the structural surface beneath the roof covering.

What information should I give a roofing repair contractor in Linden?
Share the property address, where you noticed the issue, and when it first came to your attention. If you know of earlier roof work, mention that too, but let the contractor assess the cause.
Does every roof concern need the same kind of repair?
No. The appropriate work depends on what is found at the property and how much of the roof is affected. Ask the contractor to explain the findings and the reason for the recommended scope.
