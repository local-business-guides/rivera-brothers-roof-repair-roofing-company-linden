How to understand the work and its cost

A contractor typically needs to understand the roof concern before describing a suitable scope. The details may include the affected area, materials, access, and whether related parts of the roof need attention. Ask what the proposed work covers and how it connects to what was observed.

Key facts for your discussion:
- What it covers: Confirm whether the plan addresses one spot or a wider section, and ask what is excluded.
- Typical timing: There is no single schedule for every roofing job. The work type, size, roof condition, access, and other job details can all affect timing.
- General cost factors: Roof dimensions and pitch, material choices, access, damage, and the work included can influence cost. Ask for the estimate to explain its scope and main factors.
- Useful terms: *Flashing* helps cover roof joints and transitions. *Decking* is the underlying roof surface. A *roof leak* describes water entering through the roofing system, but the visible spot may not explain its source.
